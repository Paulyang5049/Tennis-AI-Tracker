Before accepting a source, inspect the actual video locally, record its SHA-256 and original match identity, verify reuse terms at the original source, and document whether participants and recording owners permit analysis and publication. Keep downloaded media and annotations outside version control. Do not infer model accuracy from provided labels until the label definitions and independence are audited.

## CalTennis spot check, 29 September 2026

The public `metadata_mini.jsonl` index has 38 video rows across six session IDs. Its paths require checking against the actual repository tree: one indexed September 18 `SW` MP4 returned 404 and was absent from that session's file listing. Remote `ffprobe` found the September 18 `ESE` file to be 366.997 seconds; a frame at 180 seconds showed a half-court view with one player. The September 18 overhead file is 4600.878 seconds and shows the full court, but frames at 1800 and 3000 seconds showed one player and no players, respectively. The January 23 `E` and January 28 `X` sampled frames also showed partial-court views. The October 2 overhead file is 4790.728 seconds and a frame at 1200 seconds showed a full court with two players. A still frame cannot establish that a continuous 5–10 minute excerpt contains singles match play, nor whether another session is a distinct original match. The October 2 view merits further screening, but these checks leave **zero verified eligible matches**. The sampled frames and video remain outside the repository; no figure-use decision follows from this inspection.

The broader `metadata_mid.jsonl` index has 39 more video rows across ten session IDs, again without a match/practice label. Sampled frames from its February 24 `W` view at 600 seconds and March 11 `W` view at 300 seconds showed partial-court views with more than one person on the same side. This does not rule out every other view in those sessions, but the broader index also does not establish a distinct-match, full-court singles cohort. None of these records changes the verified acquisition count.
