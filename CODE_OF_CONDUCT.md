# Community Code of Conduct

Tennis AI Local welcomes tennis players, coaches, researchers, designers and developers of every experience level. Treat other contributors with patience, respect and curiosity.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, constructive feedback about ideas and work.
- Respect privacy, consent and ownership of tennis footage and annotations.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar technical language.
- Credit data, code, research and creative work accurately.
- Focus disagreement on evidence and project outcomes.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct and publishing private footage or information without consent are not acceptable.

To report conduct that cannot be handled safely in a public issue, contact the repository owner through the private contact option on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed privately and handled in proportion to the situation. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
